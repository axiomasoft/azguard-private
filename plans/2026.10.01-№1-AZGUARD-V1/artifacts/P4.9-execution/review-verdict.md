**GREEN.** No acceptance-blocking defect in the inspected P4.9 implementation.

`decideMany` keeps scalar parity on the paths that were inspected. One `now` is fixed in `Authorizer::decideMany`. Results are restored with `ksort` before `DecisionSet::of`. The recursion key includes the permission, so a later different permission still runs, and the same panel, subject, permission, and scope still throws. Policies, restrictions, conditions, and before/after run per request; resource clones are not memoized.

Grants groups share one `ReadAttempt` per panel, subject, and tenant. Contexts are read in chunks of at most 100 between one `T_before` and one `T_after`, and a mismatch retries the whole group at most three times. `ReadAttemptChanged` and a cross-group `DecisionSet` conflict use `discardedFrame`, so a discarded attempt does not keep a `StateToken`. `after` runs only on the attempt that is returned. PolicyOnly stays off that fence. A static before-deny is kept when a neighbor's authority read fails. Dynamic catalog reads and assignment reads share the attempt. State map keys stay `IdentityCodec::compose(['code', panel, buildId])` and `compose(['storage', storageId, panel])`.

Host reads are set-based: subject `whereKey` chunks, queryable scope `whereKey` chunks plus `tenantOf`, and eligibility `UNION ALL` with a per-witness tag and the nested structural query. Non-queryable scopes call `resolve` once per panel and ref. External checks go through `allowsMany`. Role and grant field witnesses stay in the eligibility key.
